Welcome To CPEC Saint Babeth

CPEC Saint Babeth is an Ordinary Level and Technical Secondary School dedicated to providing quality education and practical skills for students.

Our mission is to prepare students for a successful future through discipline, creativity, innovation, and modern technology.

We offer strong academic programs together with practical training in Software Development and Multimedia.

At CPEC Saint Babeth, students gain knowledge, confidence, leadership skills, and hands-on experience for the modern world.
